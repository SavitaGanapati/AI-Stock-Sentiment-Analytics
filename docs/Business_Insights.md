# Business Insights & Value

## Business Problem

Financial-news teams often face a large volume of fragmented information
with inconsistent structure.

The platform turns that information into a consistent analytical layer.

## Business Value

### 1. Faster information discovery

Instead of manually scanning multiple sources, users can explore
consolidated stock and sector intelligence.

### 2. Context-rich sentiment

Sentiment is combined with:

-   ticker/entity;
-   sector;
-   theme;
-   confidence;
-   model agreement.

### 3. Sector-level visibility

Users can identify sectors receiving positive, neutral or negative news
attention.

### 4. Theme-level visibility

The system can surface recurring market narratives such as:

-   IPO;
-   crude oil;
-   interest rates;
-   regulatory developments;
-   geopolitical risk;
-   quarterly results.

### 5. Drill-down capability

Aggregate metrics can be connected back to article-level evidence.

## Example Decision Flow

``` text
Market theme
     ↓
Sector affected
     ↓
Stocks receiving coverage
     ↓
News sentiment
     ↓
Model confidence/agreement
     ↓
Analyst investigation
```

## Important Business Boundary

The platform is designed for **research and decision support**.

It does not claim that:

``` text
Positive sentiment = guaranteed price increase
```

or:

``` text
Negative sentiment = guaranteed price decrease
```

The value is in organizing and contextualizing information for further
analysis.
